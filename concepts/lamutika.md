# LamutikA: the originating process language

LamutikA is the conceptual framework from which the language approaches in MeSH’lAng developed. It works with operators, directional modulation and composition: an expression is intended to unfold into a process or structure.

LamutikA combines semantic process description and an operative, mathematical layer in one process language. dRy and crY develop these emphases as separate language forms. This relationship does not make their operator definitions interchangeable.

Its underlying axiomatic framework remains private. The public examples are usable through their stated definitions; the [script rationale](semantic-addressing.md#why-a-shared-writing-system-matters) explains the aesthetic and practical choice.

For a new reader, its important role here is straightforward. A name or sequence can describe *how something proceeds*. A model interprets that description under a language definition and a task context.

## How the family relates

- **moQ’eX** makes task and workflow structure directly readable through familiar operations.
- **dRy** develops semantic orientation and addressable State/Trajectory expressions.
- **crY** develops mathematically oriented operations and architectural composition.
- **Bry** records a Bengali-script exploration within the same family; this edition supplies no full profile.

They explore dedicated ways of addressing different layers and composable spectra of a model's semantic space. The [naming lineage](names.md#language-names-and-capitalization) records crY's crystallization inspiration, dRy's development from it and the Bry exploration. Their specific operator definitions remain distinct.

The [name renderings](names.md) show how a process-oriented reading can be expressed in ordinary prose. They are interpretation drafts, rather than complete canonical definitions.

## From expression to implementation

The [Webstuhl example](../case-studies/webstuhl.md) starts with separate structural, functional and interface expressions. Those expressions are intended to unfold into modules, operations and their connections.

Webstuhl is an application architecture. Geotopie and context-delivery hooks supply surrounding memory and integration mechanisms. They are not additional synonyms for LamutikA or MeSH’lAng.

LamutikA remains in development and is not released here. This publication records its lineage and selected interpretative references; it does not distribute the language itself, its complete corpus or full algebraic definitions.
