# Bry

Bry is a Bengali-script exploration within MeSH’lAng. The **B** points to Bengali. Its name participates in the deliberate capitalization sequence **crY → dRy → Bry**, which moves the capital letter one position at a time.

The developer describes having conceived and explored this line alongside the other language forms. This edition records that reported work; it does not include a runnable Bry profile, operator inventory, teaching primer or benchmark dataset.

## Place in the family

The family explores different, potentially composable spectra of semantic addressing. Bry investigates another writing-system frame within that approach. Its name does not establish that replacing dRy glyphs with Bengali characters preserves the same readings: a usable language line needs its own defined bindings and teaching context.

See [the naming lineage](../concepts/names.md#language-names-and-capitalization), [semantic spectra](../concepts/semantic-addressing.md#composable-semantic-spectra) and [the originating framework](../concepts/lamutika.md).
