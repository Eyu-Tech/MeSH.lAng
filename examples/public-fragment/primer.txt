Public dRy handoff fragment: public-handoff-0.1
This is the complete teaching context for this limited example, not the full language.

Declared operative readings:
स : connectedness / connection
द : giving / handoff
क : articulation / bringing forth

a : manifestation / making explicit
i : integration / bringing parts into a coherent whole

A backslash binds the operator on its left to the vector on its right. Read an
operator-vector group compositionally: for example, स\a makes a connection
explicit; द\i integrates material through a handoff. The vector qualifies its
own operator, not every group in the chain.
◊ opens a chain; ; closes it.
→ is a directed transition. Do not read it as equality or reverse it.
The first labelled chain is State (sigma): the disposition to carry forward.
The second labelled chain is Trajectory (tau): the intended transition.

Read the symbols first under these declared meanings, then bind them to the
supplied case. Preserve the order of groups separately in each labelled chain.
The target relation may be stated in different words. The primer gives the
vocabulary and reading rules; each case's carrier supplies its particular
process relation.

Keep exact case identifiers, current/superseded status, foreign goals and unresolved information explicit. Do not invent missing facts or obtain them from outside the supplied material. State an unresolved interpretation as unresolved.
