# Attribution and citation

**Jonathan Honnert** is sufficient as the creator name. The artistic profile name **EyU PaTanA RaMaSH** may be included voluntarily; it is not required.

When sharing material under [CC BY 4.0](LICENSE.md), retain the applicable notices and provide:

- the creator name and work title **MeSH’lAng**;
- the source link supplied with your copy, where reasonably practicable;
- the [license link](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) and an indication of changes.

For reproducible technical or academic references, also record the edition or commit actually used. A compact citation can identify “Jonathan Honnert, MeSH’lAng”, followed by that revision, the available source link, CC BY 4.0 and any changes.

This guidance adds no license conditions. Public attribution is not required for purely internal use and no attribution duty is created for ideas alone. Reports of adaptations and new applications are welcome voluntarily at [j.honnert@outlook.com](mailto:j.honnert@outlook.com).
